Technical Services

AC/DC Motor Overhaul & Rewinding

At Futurion, we understand that motors are the beating heart of your industrial operations. We provide comprehensive repair, rewinding, and overhauling services for all types of AC and DC motors. Our state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and industry-leading expertise ensure your critical rotational equipment returns to optimal efficiency and reliability.

Electric motors represent a significant capital investment and are critical to maintaining uptime across energy, water treatment, manufacturing, and process industries. Whether you operate massive high-voltage AC synchronous motors or intricate DC traction motors, unexpected failures can paralyze your production lines.

Futurion's dedicated motor repair facilities are equipped to handle everything from routine preventative maintenance and bearing replacements to complete stator core restacking and custom armature rewinding. We utilize Class H insulation materials as a standard, ensuring superior thermal resistance and prolonged operational lifespan under extreme industrial conditions.

Comprehensive Motor Solutions

Our multi-disciplinary engineering approach guarantees that a repaired motor often outperforms its original factory specifications. We do not just fix the symptom; our root-cause analysis protocols identify why the failure occurred in the first place—be it electrical tracking, mechanical vibration, thermal degradation, or environmental contamination.

  • Mechanical Repairs: Precision shaft manufacturing, bearing housing sleeve installations, dynamic balancing, and fan/blower refurbishment.
  • Electrical Rewinding: Complete redesign and rewinding of AC stators, DC armatures, and field coils using VIP (Vacuum Pressure Impregnation) techniques.
  • Advanced Diagnostics: Surge testing, core loss testing, Megger and PI polarization index tests, and advanced thermography analysis.

Specialized Overhaul Capabilities

AC Motor (Induction & Synchronous)

We expertly service low, medium, and high-voltage AC motors. Our overhaul includes detailed electrical profiling, re-wedging, dip-and-bake varnishing, rotor bar inspection via non-destructive testing, and complete mechanical tolerances restoration. We also service specialized flameproof and explosion-proof (Ex-d) motors used in hazardous oil and gas environments.

DC Motor & Traction Overhaul

DC motors require highly specialized attention. Futurion provides precision commutator turning, undercutting, and profiling. We handle brush gear refurbishment, interpoles, and rigorous megger testing of armature circuitry. Our facility guarantees that sparkless neutral planes are set perfectly before the motor is dispatched.

Why Trust Futurion for Motor Repairs?

OEM Standard Compliance

Our overhaul procedures adhere strictly to OEM guidelines and international engineering standards (EASA / IEEE). We ensure the repaired motor aligns with its original torque, efficiency, and temperature curves.

VPI Technology

We utilize advanced Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI) for stator and armature rewinding. This process completely eliminates air voids in the insulation, drastically increasing dielectric strength and mechanical rigidity.

Dynamic Balancing

Every rotor and armature undergoes strict computerized dynamic balancing testing up to operating speeds. Eliminating out-of-balance forces ensures significantly longer bearing life and reduced machine vibration.
