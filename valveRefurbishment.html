Safety Valve Testing Process

Our rigorous 8-step protocol ensures absolute safety and compliance.

01

Pre-Test

Valve mounted on test bench, initial performance recorded.

02

Disassemble

Stripped down to component form for deep inspection.

03

Inspection

All components inspected; condition logged for report.

04

Overhaul

Cleaning, lapping of seat/disk, and ensuring free movement.

05

Reassemble

Precision rebuilding of the valve assembly.

06

Set & Test

Pressure setting and bubble/back-pressure tests (API/ASME).

07

Protection

Inlet/outlet ports protected to prevent ingress during shipping.

08

Certification

Final tagging and issuance of Certified Test Certificate.
