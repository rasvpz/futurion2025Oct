Equipment reliability is a continuous journey. Futurion's After Sales Support team is dedicated to extending the operational life of every product and system we supply or service. Through proactive maintenance programs, rapid spare-parts sourcing, and on-site technical assistance, we minimize unplanned downtime and maximize your return on investment.

Whether you require a routine inspection, an emergency repair, or in-depth operator training, our expert engineers are available across the Middle East to respond quickly and effectively — because your uptime is our priority.

Our After Sales Services

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled, systematic servicing of your pumps, motors, valves, and generators to detect issues before they cause costly failures and unplanned shutdowns.

Emergency Breakdown Support

Round-the-clock emergency response with mobilisation of qualified engineers to your site within 24 hours — restoring operations with minimal disruption.

Spare Parts Supply

Fast-track procurement of OEM and compatible spare parts from our trusted global network, ensuring genuine components reach you with minimal lead time.

Operator Training

Structured on-site and classroom training programmes that empower your plant operators with the knowledge to safely and efficiently manage installed equipment.

Performance Audits

In-depth operational audits and diagnostic assessments to benchmark equipment efficiency, identify performance gaps, and recommend corrective actions.

Service Agreements (AMC)

Flexible Annual Maintenance Contracts tailored to your operational needs, providing priority access to our engineering teams, planned service visits, and cost certainty.

How We Work

Contact & Assessment

Reach out via phone, email, or our service portal. Our team quickly assesses your request and assigns the right specialist.

Diagnosis & Planning

Our engineers carry out detailed on-site or remote diagnostics and prepare a transparent, prioritised action plan with timelines and cost estimates.

Execution & Repair

Qualified technicians carry out repairs, replacements, or maintenance using OEM-approved methods and rigorously tested components.

Testing & Sign-off

Post-service performance testing and comprehensive documentation provided, along with operator handover to confirm full operational readiness.

What We Offer?

  • Dedicated Support Engineers: Named account engineers who understand your specific equipment, site conditions, and operational history — delivering consistent, high-quality support.
  • Lifecycle Management: End-to-end management of your equipment lifecycle — from warranty support through to planned replacement strategies — ensuring maximum asset return.
  • Condition Monitoring: Advanced vibration analysis, thermographic scanning, and oil analysis to track equipment health trends and predict failures before they occur.
  • Warranty Administration: Seamless management of all warranty claims and in-warranty service activities, coordinating directly with OEM manufacturers on your behalf.
